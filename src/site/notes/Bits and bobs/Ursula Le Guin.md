Those that walk away from Omeleas is short story that leaves a pick of ice in the heart. Ice truly as it chills you then melts and drips into your capillaries. What is about this simple story that leaves to such metaphors. 

We have a utopia a beautiful city full of all wonders that we can imagine. Boundless joy and painless living on streets that parade with happy people living free. In it's centre a child is locked in dark room. Dirty, naked, abused and forgotten. Shackled to the wall. 

Everyone in the city of Omeleas knows that this child is here. They visit them on a school tour. They are told that this child is required to suffer for the good and the joy of the city to be maintained. The question that you must ask is what would you do? 

Many give a range of gut reactions and say they would free the child would tear down the city. Would set off into the wilderness. 

We already live in this world. But there are many more children and many more rooms. What will you do? Do you walk away?
