You can’t organise an orgy this is to say pleasure is picking brambles in the garden of a sleeping giant. 

We can pick a place and a time sure but joy and pleasure is a thing that sneaks up on us while we are trying to do something else, a chance encounter, a stolen kiss, the crush you love to hate, a one night stand, a dark room an unexpected combination of sensations, words or flavours. 

I’m going to let you in on a little secret. Every day, once a day, give yourself a present. Don't plan it, don't wait for it, just let it happen, a catnap in your office chair or two cups of good hot black coffee. Like this.

 As soon as we plan it, put it on a spread sheet or god forbid a gant chart and try to force it -pleasure evaporates. The google calendar is for pain and pleasure is in the gaps between the optimised life in the unconscious when you let the soft animal of your body love what it loves. 

When El , Alex, Philly and I started talking about this meal and aphrodisiacs I was resistant because when we think of aphrodisiacs we often think of a heart shaped box of easily consumable sensuality but the more I thought about it the more I became entranced by how little Irish culture speaks about pleasure. Every fairy story is about not running away with yourself. 
You must not eat
You must not fuck
You must not fall in love 
but if you do your feet must never touch the ground

Desire is an underground if you look at what has been considered an aphrodisiac; it is nearly every single plant or produce imaginable: rule 34 for food. Nearly every aphrodisiac is directed at the masculine body as well. So many songs and stories which dealt with sex were neatly cut out of the record or censored by monks or antiquarians. Desire is everywhere just below the surface. 

Queer pleasure and desire is a basement below the basement. The love that dare not speak its name. No wonder pride is often presented as so one dimensionally because the idea that we can love and feel joy outside the bounds of the traditional social orders will always be a threat to the status quo. 

 The pleasure of the other, the pure joy of food and sex, exists behind a vale happening but unacknowledged. We make noise when we fuck and when we eat not because we intend to but because we can’t help it - because we lose ourself in a moment out of time or we are faking it.


Today we want to have some treats and have a little chat about what a life more keenly in touch with queer pleasure might look like. I will stop talking now but just to leave you with something here is a little quote from José Esteban Muñoz



Queerness is not yet here…The here and now is a prison house. We must strive, in the face of the here and now's totalizing rendering of reality, to think and feel a then and there. Some will say that all we have are the pleasures of the moment, but we must never settle for that minimal transport; we must dream and enact new and better pleasures, other ways of being in the world, and ultimately new worlds ... Queerness is essentially about the rejection of a here and now and an insistence on potentiality or concrete possibility for another world."


-- Cruising Utopia: The Then and There of Queer Futurity
